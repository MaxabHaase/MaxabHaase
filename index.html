I’m an evolutionary cell biologist studying how centromeres and kinetochores originate, diversify, and assemble at the molecular level. Using the remarkable natural diversity of budding yeasts, I investigate how chromosome-segregation systems evolve while preserving their essential function.

I’m currently a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellow with Andrea Musacchio at the Max Planck Institute of Molecular Physiology in Dortmund. My current work combines native biochemistry and structural biology with comparative genomics and genetics to understand how a single budding-yeast kinetochore is built—and what this minimal system can teach us about the larger, multi-attachment centromeres found in other eukaryotes.
